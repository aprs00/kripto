### Think-through 1: T2b(e) listing_short liquidity hysteresis (cost, HL main's lead leg)
(a) Mechanism: not a signal. One threshold on a noisy 7-d dollar volume makes coins near it flip in and out; each flip pays a round trip on the
thinnest coins (live XTEN: 60 bp in, 22 bp out, one day held, ≈ 1% of the book). Hysteresis pays nothing for noise. Side effect: it keeps
shorts in coins whose volume is dying (the decay we want to hold, but exits get dearer and squeezes thinner).
(b) Source: our own live fills (review 11-18). The backtest charges 3·√(50M/dv) bp = 21 bp at dv $1M, live paid 60: the model UNDER-prices
threshold names, so a backtest understates any saving here (bias against the change, which is the safe direction).
(c) Forms: A base (enter/stay 1M); B hysteresis (enter 1M, stay 0.5M); C entry floor (enter 2M, stay 1M); D hold-through (enter 1M, stay 0).
B is closest to the backlog idea; C trades fewer, thinner events; D is the extreme of B.
(d) Fail modes unrelated to the idea: too few threshold crossings to move a Sharpe (then it is a live-cost question, not a backtest one); the
cost model's thin-name bias hides the saving; D/B holding dead coins whose volume vanished (delisting risk on bnall).
(e) Mind-changers: churn events < 5% of entries on both venues → not worth a change; a variant that lowers SR > 0.1 on held-out → reject.
Explore = bnall up to 2023-06-30 (events and returns). Confirm, untouched until the rule is written: bnall 2023-07-01 onward AND HL in full.
